### Carleton University Mechatronics Society (TRONSoc) Workshop Repository

Welcome to the repository for TRONSoc workshop materials. This serves as a central location for our past and present workshop materials and projects.

---

## About TRONSoc

TRONSoc is Carleton University's official student society for Mechatronics Engineering. We are dedicated to advocating for students in the mechatronics program, spreading interest in mechatronics and robotics across Carleton Engineering, and connecting students with faculty, the robotics industry, and each other. 

---

## Repository Contents

### 1. Workshops (`/workshops`)
This directory contains our educational resources like tutorial documents and final products for every workshop we've hosted.

* For Example: **`workshops/bobaCAD/`** Contains Finished CAD models and tutorial documents from all three BobaCAD 2026 workshops.

---

### 2. Current Projects (`/projects`)
This section serves as a resource hub for ongoing engineering projects developed by TRONSoc members. 

Each project subfolder can include:
* CAD models and assemblies.
* Firmware and software.
* Electrical schematics, PCB designs, and the project's Bill of Materials (BOM).
